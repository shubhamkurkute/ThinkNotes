### What is Agentic AI?
- Agentic AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that can operate autonomously, make decisions, and interact with the environment without constant human intervention.
- These AI systems are designed to perceive, reason and act to achieve specific objectives. They often collaborative with other agents to complete complex tasks efficiently.

### Agentic AI: Key Characteristics
**Adaptabality:** It adjusts to new inputs and environments with minimal human intervention.
**Autonomy:** It operates independently and collaborates with other agents.
**Decision-making:** It learns from past experiences and refines its reasoning.
**Collaboration:** It uses cognitive processing to analyze information and execute effectively.
